## Corrected merge blocker

**P2: hand-size experimental variants reused the baseline thumb input.**

PR commit `87b36b0` made `profiles._finalise` preserve an explicitly supplied nonzero thumb dimension. That preservation is useful, but the existing `ergo_experiments.pool_for` starts with an already finalized synthetic baseline. Updating only its `hand_percentile` therefore left the actual reach input unchanged for the small- and large-hand conditions.

A direct call to the existing generator on the reviewed head produced:

| Synthetic condition | Declared percentile | Before correction | After correction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Small hand | 5 | 107.0 mm | 96.3 mm |
| Baseline | 50 | 107.0 mm | 107.0 mm |
| Large hand | 95 | 107.0 mm | 117.7 mm |

These numbers are the repository's synthetic model inputs, not measured anatomy or a validated Korean population distribution. Equal inputs made the intended hand-size comparison ineffective.

The correction resets the generator's existing internal derivation sentinel only when that synthetic experimental condition explicitly overrides `hand_percentile`. It does not overwrite an externally authored dimension, alter the NVIDIA seed rows, replace the reach model, or change historical analyses.

- `skills/remorsearch-ux-qa/scripts/ergo_experiments.py`

The added regression calls the existing experiment generator for portrait and landscape grips. It checks that small, baseline and large conditions have ordered, corresponding reach inputs, and that an unrelated walking condition retains its baseline size.
